Platform, App & Device

The @capacitor/core runtime plus the Device, App, Network and Motion plugins. Native-only calls report a graceful error in the browser.

Preferences, Cookies & Clipboard

Key/value storage with Preferences, cookie management through CapacitorCookies, and the Clipboard copy/paste pair.

Filesystem

The whole Filesystem surface: write, read (including chunked reads), append, directories, stat/copy/rename, URIs and downloads inside the app's data directory.

Camera & Barcode scanner

TakePhoto, ChooseFromGallery, RecordVideo, PlayVideo and the permission flow, plus BarcodeScanner.ScanBarcode. The camera preview uses the photo's webPath.

Geolocation

One-shot and watched positions, including the new interval/minimumUpdateInterval options.

Dialogs, toast & haptics

Dialog (alert/confirm/prompt), Toast, Haptics and ActionSheet.

Notifications

LocalNotifications scheduling, the pending/delivered queries (including the 8.3 getAll/getByIds and update/cancelAll methods), channels, and the PushNotifications registration flow.

Share & browsers

Share, Browser, AppLauncher and the full InAppBrowser option model.

System UI & platform plugins

Controls that only do something in a native shell: StatusBar, Keyboard, ScreenOrientation, ScreenReader, TextZoom, SplashScreen, BiometricAuth, BackgroundRunner and GoogleMaps.

HTTP

CapacitorHttp — the native-backed HTTP client that falls back to fetch on the web. Requests api.github.com/zen (which allows CORS).

Activity log

Every call above reports here; failures on the web are expected for native-only APIs.
